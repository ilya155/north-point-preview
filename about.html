About North Point Services

A family-owned Minnesota contractor focused on quality workmanship and responsive project management.

Who we are

Trusted locally. Built for lasting work.

North Point Services Co. is a family-owned roofing and exterior/interior contractor based in Lakeville, Minnesota. We serve the Minneapolis area and surrounding counties with commercial and residential projects.

Our positioning is straightforward: licensed and certified practices, decades of experience, quality workmanship, and a one-stop approach that covers both exterior systems and interior improvements.

Clients often work directly with our owner and project manager, Alex (also known as Aleksei or Aleksey) Zaytsev — known for responsive communication from estimate through completion.
